### About the product management for designers course

This course helps UX designers, UI designers, and product designers learn the commercial and analytical skills of product management and align their designs with the organization’s revenue and growth goals.

---

### Key benefits of joining:

- **A deep grasp of commercial goals and revenue models in the design process**: Connect user research to business metrics (retention, conversion).
- **Smooth collaboration with product managers and the engineering team**: Learn the language of data and strategy.
- **The ability to prioritize design ideas with agile validation methods**.
- **Expanding the role from UI/UX Designer to Product Designer and Product Lead**.

---

### Course syllabus:

1. Product management for designers: the intersection of user, business, and technology
2. Analyzing user behavior and product metrics with quantitative and qualitative data
3. Design sprints and rapid prototyping to validate assumptions
4. Formulating value proposition and designing the customer journey map
5. Prioritizing capabilities and managing project scope
6. Conversion rate optimization (CRO) and reducing churn by improving design
7. Presenting and defending design decisions to stakeholders and senior leaders
