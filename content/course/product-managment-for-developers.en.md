### About the product management for developers course

This course is designed for software engineers, developers, and technical leads so they can learn a product mindset, bridge from a purely coding view to customer value and business, and play a key role in strategic decisions.

---

### Benefits of joining:

- **Align technical development with commercial goals**: Understand why features are built and avoid shipping unused code.
- **Better collaboration with designers, product managers, and executives**: Master a shared language with non-technical teams.
- **More influence on architecture and product prioritization**: Analyze technical debt against commercial value.
- **Preparation to move into Tech Lead, Engineering Manager, or Product Manager**.

---

### Syllabus and lesson titles:

1. Product thinking vs. project thinking: what does the product manager want?
2. Software economics basics, revenue models, and key performance indicators (KPIs)
3. Requirements analysis, writing user stories, and breaking down tasks around user value
4. Working effectively with product designers and understanding UX principles
5. Prioritizing capabilities and managing the backlog with agile methods
6. Testing, experiments, validating assumptions, and continuous product improvement
7. Career path and how to move from software development into product management
