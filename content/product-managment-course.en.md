A complete guide to starting as a product manager in an organization

The product and business management course is 6 practical weeks for learning and practicing product management so you can apply it in a new organization. The course is based on The Product Manager’s Survival Guide, and other people’s experience is used to go deeper into the book’s ideas.

### Why a product management course

Product management is one of the ideas that has been added to the software-development process in recent years. Product management is an interdisciplinary job (the intersection of engineering, user experience, and business teams). In interdisciplinary jobs, gaining enough knowledge and skill in every area takes time.

In that situation, taking training courses and learning from experienced people helps you gain current knowledge grounded in real-world experience.

There also needs to be a connection between what is taught in class and the real experiences and problems of businesses, so the teaching settles in participants’ minds.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long is this course?**

The course has 11 chapters and takes 11 weeks. After that there is one more week for wrap-up and a final exam.

**Who is this course for?**

This course is for people who have already learned the core product-management skills, have had a short stint in a role, and want to join a new company and pull their experience and learning together.

**Who is a product manager?**

Product management is responsible for the product’s success across its entire lifecycle. The product manager is the link between company leadership and the product-development team, and the approach is to identify and specify the organization’s goals and match them to the product. A product manager offers a unique product vision based on customer needs and market analysis.

**Will I be able to work in a specialist way with the product-management software named above?**

Teaching in this course is at the level of getting to know the tools. You will need to go deeper later into whichever tools you need more.
