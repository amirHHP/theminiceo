In answer to the question of how you can penetrate the market with a new product, it must be said that the best work is for a company to use the full potential of its sales team and involve them in creating product positioning, the promotion plan, and the go-to-market strategy.

A successful strategy for penetrating, marketing, and selling new products involves its sales team at every stage.

When selling in a crowded market, your sales strategy is the foundation on which your success will be built. In this article, we examined how your sellers can excel using relationship-focused sales techniques.

#### Involve your sellers in this work

Your sellers play an essential role in the process of positioning the product and marketing it. They know your target customer and have built meaningful relationships with the customer.

Encourage your sellers to gain knowledge and understanding of competitors, how products are arranged, and the strategies they use. This information is very valuable for establishing the position of a new product.

#### Train your sales team

Launching a new product is always anxiety-inducing. Every member of the sales team must put the products on display, so they must fully understand its features and benefits. This helps them find solutions for sales problems with confidence.

> Help your sellers look for learning themselves. Explain the what, why, and how of new products to sellers. Note that sales managers need a coach, training, and an environment that encourages them to take responsibility for their own learning.

Ask your sellers to evaluate the production stages and ask what is useful and practical and what is not, and combine their best experiences and methods to produce a sales strategy for the new product that reflects its value and enables them to communicate with customers.

#### Create an environment for sharing

Focus on enabling sellers to share their experiences of success and failure in selling the new product. In an environment that encourages continuous learning, provide the method, place, and time for exchanging information among sellers.

#### Create new opportunities for existing customers

A value proposition helps sellers target current customers and leads to guiding customers who may churn and need re-engagement to retain them.

#### Creating a position for the product

Using your sellers’ knowledge and experience, you will be able to design a strategy that achieves the three components of creating a position for the product:

- Differentiate your new product from your competitors
- Respond to customers’ needs
- Explain the main features of the new product

#### Product promotion

With your product-positioning strategy defined and clear, you will be ready to create your marketing plan.

Make noise on social media; combine this with email messaging and traditional advertising and advertising at trade shows. All of your marketing materials and messages should:

- Convey a single core message
- Connect with the target customer
- Be different from competing products
- Be trustworthy and believable, and offer a sustainable solution

Many of the companies we examined have not been able to fully use their sellers’ ability in the early stages of penetrating the market with a new product.

However, the necessity of new products’ success is including sellers in strategic conversations at the earliest possible stage.

#### Product penetration

With positioning and promotion now in place, it is time to go to market. Now it is time to go back to the basics. This is where [the seven characteristics of a successful salesperson](http://www.forwardfocusinc.com/ignite-sales/sales-mindset-101-the-7-characteristics-of-a-successful-salesperson/) fully [appear](http://www.forwardfocusinc.com/ignite-sales/sales-mindset-101-the-7-characteristics-of-a-successful-salesperson/).

Selling a new product is done easily by a seller who is liked and trusted and has built a strong business relationship with customers. When strong sales skills are combined with high emotional intelligence, the result is a powerful sales technique that ignites sales.

Sales managers must make sure that their sales teams have understood the new product, how it is positioned in the market, and its main benefits for new and existing customers.

So sales managers have the duty to provide the necessary training on launching the new product. Because ultimately it is the sales team that makes success happen.

There will still be resistance to “testing” a new product. Sellers who have enough skill in earning trust, and not only with needs and wants but better skill in communicating, will be able to bring sales to the level they should:

Access to an excellent new product in the early stages

By requesting valuable customer feedback and listening to them before taking the right actions, your company will be in the best position to develop its product-placement and promotion strategies to fully use the market’s real potential.
