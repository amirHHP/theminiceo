### 📖 About the Book & Core Premise

When I started teaching product management in a very limited way on TheMiniCEO (TMC), I realized that this kind of teaching only really helps people who want to start the path from zero. Once professional work begins, things get complicated enough that training alone no longer cuts it. The daily challenges that are specific to that company, industry, and set of users are not written in books. I myself face challenges every day that I do not have a ready solution for, which is why the idea of a product managers’ club came to me: a place where people can talk comfortably, share their challenges, and ask others to think with them about solving them. And yes, I am a customer and user of this club myself.

The club’s capacity is 8 people, and the monthly membership fee is 500,000 Toman, which is spent on maintaining the page, communications, coordination, and similar work.

(The sessions are held privately, but they are recorded, and at the end anyone who wants can ask for a given part of what they said to be removed.)

Registering does not mean automatic acceptance. After you register we will message you, because we will probably have several groups, and it matters that people at similar levels are in similar groups. The challenge of someone with one or two months of product experience is not something a senior can usefully solve, because they see that world in a different way.
