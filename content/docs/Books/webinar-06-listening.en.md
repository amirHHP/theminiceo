Listening is one of the skills a product manager (or perhaps anyone) needs for effective communication with other stakeholders. A product manager, though, because they work with many stakeholders, needs more than others to listen well, and because an important part of the basis for their decisions is information they collect from others, it matters that they receive that information in a lean way, without mental preconceptions.

In this webinar we talk with Khashayar Pakfar about lean listening. Khashayar has worked on personal-development skills for a long time and also has a strong background in product management.
