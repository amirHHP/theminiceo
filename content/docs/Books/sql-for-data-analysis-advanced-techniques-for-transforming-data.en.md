### 📖 About the Book & Core Premise

**SQL for Data Analysis Advanced Techniques for Transforming Data** by **Cathy Tanimura** offers an authoritative guide to data-informed product development, metric hierarchy design, and rigorous experimentation. The book demonstrates how to cut through vanity metrics, construct trusted telemetry pipelines, and apply statistical experimentation to prove that product iterations drive tangible commercial outcomes.

Throughout this work, Cathy Tanimura synthesizes proven methodologies and field-tested frameworks to help product practitioners solve critical challenges in analytics & data. Rather than focusing on superficial tactics, the text dives deep into the underlying mechanics of customer value, team dynamics, and strategic clarity.

Whether you are establishing new product practices or scaling established systems, this comprehensive guide serves as an indispensable manual for making principled, data-informed product decisions.

* **North Star & Input Metric Trees:** Deconstructing top-level business outcomes into actionable leading behavioral indicators that product squads can directly influence.
