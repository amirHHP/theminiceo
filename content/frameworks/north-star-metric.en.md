A **North Star Metric** is one core measure that, if chosen well, shows both the value you deliver to customers and healthy business growth. Amplitude popularized the idea for product teams: instead of ten conflicting dashboards, one number the whole team can align on.

## What a good North Star looks like

- **It measures customer value, not internal activity.** “Stories closed” is not a North Star.
- **It leads sustainable growth.** Net revenue can lag; the metric should signal earlier.
- **It is actionable.** Discovery and delivery should be able to move it.
- **It has clear inputs.** Three to five input metrics usually sit under the North Star.

Examples: Spotify’s time spent listening, Airbnb’s nights booked, Slack’s messages in teams that reached habitual use. Copying someone else’s metric is a mistake; yours should come from your own [Jobs To Be Done](/en/jobs-to-be-done/).

## A common mistake

Do not treat the North Star as a substitute for OKRs or KPIs. An OKR is a period goal; KPIs are a set of measures; the North Star is a compass. If the number rises while customers get worse off, change the metric.
