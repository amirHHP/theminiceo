## Product advisory for ideas still in your head

We help you walk the path of launching a new idea at a **much lower financial and time cost** and reach what you are looking for faster. Sometimes you go to software teams to build your idea, which can create a very large cost, while you still do not know whether the market actually needs your product. This is one of the places we can stand with you and, working with the engineering team, steer the product path so you take the least loss.

### **Your needs at this stage:**

- Maturing the idea
- Product design
- Collaboration on product development
- Taking the product to market
- Preparing the product development team
- Product growth

**What services do you get at each of these stages?**

### **Maturing the idea**

- A full review of the idea (including sessions to unpack where the idea came from and everything that can help understand it better, as well as your advantages in executing it)
- Review of a suitable space for this idea to exist
- Review of the current competitive landscape
- Review of revenue models that can be implemented and used
- A final idea proposal for the project owner

### **Product design**

- A complete list of product capabilities
- Phasing product capabilities in each version for going to market
- Preparing product sketches and wireframes
- Designing the product user interface
- Planning product MVPs to test the product in the real world
- A final proposal covering product capabilities, release phasing, UI and UX, and methods for testing different parts of the product by building an MVP

### **Collaboration on product development**

- Writing complete product user stories for implementation
- Setting up task-management tools and similar for the product team
- Planning product-team sprints
- Attending planning sessions and sprint product demos
- Reviewing the product’s marketing capabilities and implementing them (analytics tools)

### **Taking the product to market**

- Marketing planning
- Cost estimates
- Preparing marketing materials
- Help launching marketing campaigns
- Reviewing each campaign’s results and proposing improvements

### **Product growth**

- Analyzing current user behavior
- Reviewing uptake of each product capability
- Finding product weak points
- Offering different growth plans based on the owner’s needs (for example a plan to increase engagement)

## Product advisory for a startup in the growth stage

If you are a startup or a business that has been in the market for a while, and you may even have raised capital, and in this phase you need rapid growth but you are stuck, and you think the current product is one of the things blocking growth, you can use our product advisory. You have probably looked at user churn in different parts and guessed that the problem is the product and that you need to optimize it—or even change direction. Our product advisory in this area has a complete program for understanding the precise cause. Before anything else you need a professional team that identifies your product’s weak points and gives you an improvement and growth plan.

### **Your needs at this stage:**

- Identifying weak points in the current product
- Identifying quick wins
- Analyzing how product capabilities perform
- Behavioral analysis of users
- Increasing engagement, sales, and retention
- Product positioning
- Preparing the product team

**What services do you get at each of these stages?**

### **Identifying weak points in the current product**

- An initial and complete review of the current product
- Accessing existing data on user and product performance
- Running needed tests such as usability with different users
- Direct interviews with current users (if needed)

### **Identifying quick wins**

- A closer review of the product
- Tests in real conditions (A/B testing)
- Building suitable MVPs to test the product

### **Analyzing how product capabilities perform**

- Reviewing capabilities from a user-experience view
- Reviewing user drop-off at different steps of a flow
- Sometimes even deciding to remove a capability

### **Behavioral analysis of users**

- LTV of each user
- The gap between signup and becoming a customer
- Where customers churn
- User habits when using the product

### **Increasing** **engagement, retention,** **and sales**

- Running various related tests in these areas
- Collaborating with marketing on customer communication channels
- Reviewing the product revenue model

### **Product positioning**

- Help defining the position of the company/startup’s product or products
- Defining the current position
- A map of product positions in the relevant market

### **Preparing the product team**

- Reviewing and analyzing the roles the product team actually needs
- Help implementing product processes more effectively
- Structuring where product-team members sit
- Help with hiring and writing the required duties and capabilities
