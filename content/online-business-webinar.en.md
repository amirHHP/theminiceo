This course helps you quickly find an idea you are good at, based on conditions inside Iran, and find a path with the lowest cost (or no cost) to launch it.

By the end of this webinar you will be able, in less than a week, to launch your sales channel or personal website at scale, sell your product on it, and see your shop name, brand, products, and writing in Google search results.

[Enroll in the webinar](https://zarinp.al/392035)

## How this course differs from other courses

This course does not only teach you how to design and launch a site. It also covers why that business exists, the target market, expected growth, required capital, and time to return on investment. In practice you launch a real business and you know exactly, for a given amount of capital, after how long, how much you will take out.

## Syllabus:

- Finding the most suitable domain for launching an internet business based on interest, market capacity, and personal capacity
- Analyzing the idea’s market and the size of the income opportunity
- Building a prototype at the lowest possible cost
- Identifying sales channels and customer communication
- Finding the value proposition and problem–solution fit
- Presenting the different phases of product development
- Finding the business cost structure
- Finding the business revenue structure
- A basic introduction to the rules of starting a business
- Key points on buying a domain and hosting, and how to obtain them in practice
- Installing and launching a website and editing its look and different sections
- Setting up a shop and placing products in it
- Optimizing the site and raising its rank in Google
- The settings you need to write good content on the site and to optimize site products so they appear in Google search results
