![](images/JTBD-1.png)

A job to be done, or JTBD, is the progress a person hopes to make in their life. A “job” is what a person is really trying to get done in a given situation. In short, this framework says that a user uses your product to do a particular job, meet a need, or reach a specific goal—not because of the way you happen to offer it. You need to find the purpose for which your users use your product.

The idea is that people hire products or services to get a particular job done in their lives. It is then the product manager’s and marketing manager’s job to understand what job the customer is hiring them for, and how they can shape their offer to meet those needs.

A job goes beyond a functional task. There is often a social or emotional element to a job, and that is why someone chooses one product over another.
