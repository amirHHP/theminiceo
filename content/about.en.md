TheMiniCEO is a group of product specialists that started in summer 2018 to advise on leading and growing products.

With more than five years of experience among the founders and advisors, the group is a strong guide for small and mid-size businesses facing product and commercial problems.

TheMiniCEO advisors believe that solving a business problem needs a whole-business, top-down view. They use their own experience to find ways for products to move forward.

## How to use TheMiniCEO services

You may fall into one of these groups:

### You have a one-line idea

Many of our successful projects started as a one-line idea. You can try to execute any idea that comes to mind, but successful execution needs product-management skill. We turn your ideas into precise, shippable products whose chance of success is many times higher than when they were only a simple idea. All we need is that one sentence; leave the rest to TheMiniCEO specialists. Your idea is fully analyzed and its market is reviewed. Then we look at the roadmap and the lowest-cost way to implement it, and work begins. After that, with a complete business plan, you can decide how operational the idea is and what return it has at what level of capital.

1. Analysis of your current products

2. Review of your market

3. Product design / improvement

4. Support through product implementation

### You need to launch a new product

Some of our clients are organizations that want to launch a new product, already have the resources, and may even have product managers for the work, but they cannot give it enough focus because they are caught in day-to-day issues. The TheMiniCEO team joins the organization in a fully agile way, reviews the required outputs, and delivers them so you can answer the customer and the market at the lowest cost and enter the market faster.

### You need a fresh view inside the organization

Some of our clients are large companies that, for short periods, need a new, outside-the-org view injected into internal products. The TheMiniCEO team can join your company for a short window and solve problems by stepping outside stale assumptions.
